## Project Overview

This repository documents selected research and technical project deliverables prepared as part of the **Robotics and Automation Internship**. It explores the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI), intelligent sensing, autonomous decision-making, and robotic control, with particular emphasis on emerging applications in healthcare and rehabilitation.

The work combines academic research with conceptual system design to examine how intelligent robotic technologies can address practical challenges, support human capabilities, and contribute to the development of safer, more adaptive automation systems.

## Project Deliverables

### 01 — AI-Enabled Medical Robotics
**Research Report**

An academic investigation into the application of Artificial Intelligence in modern medical robotics, examining how intelligent algorithms and robotic technologies are transforming healthcare.

**Research areas include:**
- AI assisted surgical robotics and precision intervention.
- Robotic rehabilitation and post stroke recovery.
- Intelligent prosthetics and assistive robotic technologies.
- Machine learning, computer vision, and sensor integration.
- Human robot interaction and clinical decision support.
- Medical device safety, ethical considerations, and regulatory challenges.
- Emerging research directions and future opportunities.

**Deliverable:** Research report in PDF format.

### 02 — AI in Autonomous Robots
**Technical Mini-Project Proposal**

A proposed intelligent robotic rehabilitation system designed to support personalized upper limb rehabilitation following stroke.

The concept integrates multiple sensing technologies with AI-based movement assessment and adaptive robotic assistance, while retaining clinician supervision and independent safety mechanisms.

**Proposed system components:**
- Computer vision for movement observation and pose estimation.
- Inertial measurement units (IMUs) and joint position sensing.
- Force and torque sensing for interaction monitoring.
- Sensor fusion for movement state estimation.
- AI based assessment of movement quality.
- Assist as needed robotic control.
- Clinician monitoring and supervisory interface.
- Independent safety supervision and risk mitigation.

**Deliverable:** Technical mini project proposal in PDF format.

## Project Scope and Research Approach

The deliverables emphasize evidence-based research, technical feasibility, structured system design, and responsible application of intelligent automation.

The medical robotics report examines established applications alongside emerging research. The rehabilitation project is presented as a **proposed system**, not as a completed or clinically validated medical device.

Clinical deployment would require appropriate engineering verification, risk management, regulatory compliance, and clinical evaluation.

## Objectives

- Develop a stronger understanding of AI integration in robotics and automation.
- Investigate practical applications of intelligent robotic technologies.
- Explore the role of sensing, perception, and adaptive control in autonomous systems.
- Apply research findings to a healthcare focused robotic system concept.
- Consider safety, reliability, human supervision, and responsible system development.
